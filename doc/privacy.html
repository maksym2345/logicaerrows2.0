Privacy Policy

This policy was last updated on September 1, 2023.

Our privacy policy describes the personal and non-personal information that we (logic-arrows.io) collect from our users, how we collect & use it, how we protect it and your rights regarding the information you provide to us. Your use of our Web Game constitutes your agreement to our Privacy Policy.

1. Personal Information

We collect certain personal information from users, namely:

  • Email addresses
  • IP addresses

2. Non-Personal Information

We also gather browser type as part of non-personal information from our users.

3. Cookies & Tracking

Our game implements cookies, specifically for user authentication. For in-game data, we use localstorage and indexeddb.

4. Third-party services

Google OAuth is used for user authentication, which means Google may gather supplemental data. We also utilize Cloudflare for protection against DDoS attacks, and Cloudflare may accordingly collect supplemental data.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

5. Information Collection Methods

Information is collected in the following ways:

  • Email addresses are collected during user login.
  • IP addresses are collected during user login.
  • In-game data is collected as the user plays the game.

6. Usage of Collected Information

The information collected serves the following purposes:

  • Email addresses are for user authentication.
  • IP addresses are for protection against DDoS attacks.
  • In-game data is for game progress.

7. Information Sharing

The collected information is shared as follows:

  • Email addresses are shared with Google for user authentication purposes.
  • IP addresses may be shared with Cloudflare for protection against DDoS attacks.

8. Data Protection Measures

We prioritize data protection and have implemented HTTPS for all communication to ensure this. All cookies are encrypted and accessible only to the user and the server. We do not share cookies with third parties. User passwords are not stored by us.

9. Age Restrictions

Our game is not intended for children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete such information from our systems.

10. User Rights

You, as a user, uphold the rights to request your data, delete your data, or opt out of certain data collection and usage.

11. Policy Changes

Our Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time and any changes will be published on this page. Should there be significant alterations, we will notify you either via our website or in-game.
