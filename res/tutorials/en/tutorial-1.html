1. Connect two @22(Sources) to transmit signal between them.

---

2. Route the signal to the next @22.

---

3. Now you can move the camera by holding #(Mouse Wheel).

Or you can use #(Left mouse button) if there are no arrows selected.

To deselect an arrow, use #(~) key or simply click on the arrow itself in the toolbar or press the corresponding key on the keyboard.

---

4. Now you can rotate the arrows! If you have an arrow selected, it will be the one to rotate. However, if there are no arrows selected, the existing arrows on the map will be rotated.

For rotation, use the #W #A #S #D keys.

=====

1. Connect two @22(Sources) to transmit signal between them.

---

2. Route the signal to the next @22.

---

3. Now you can move the camera with #(Two fingers).

Or you can use #(One finger) if there are no arrows selected.

To deselect an arrow, tap on it in the toolbar.

---

4. Now you can rotate the arrows! To do this, switch to #(Tools) and select @i1.
